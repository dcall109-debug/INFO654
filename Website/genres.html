A guide to the sounds

Learning about different genres can help you discover more music you enjoy. Artists often combine styles, so they do not always fit into just one category.

Melodic Rap

Melodic rap combines rapping with sung melodies. Listen for how an artist moves between rapping and singing throughout a song.

R&B

R&B stands for rhythm and blues. It includes many different sounds, with melody and vocal expression often playing an important role.
