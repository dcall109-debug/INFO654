A guide to the sounds

Learning about different genres can help you discover more music you enjoy. Artists often combine styles, so they do not always fit into just one category. These are two sounds that show up throughout my rotation.

01 / Melody meets storytelling

Melodic Rap

Melodic rap combines rapping with sung melodies. Listen for how an artist moves between rhythmic verses and phrases that sound more like singing. The melody can make a song catchy, while the lyrics give you something to pay attention to on another listen.

Try “Your Friends” by Hunxho or “Ghetto Angels” by NoCap. As you listen, notice the delivery: which words are stretched into notes, and which parts follow the beat more closely? These are useful starting points for hearing how rap and melody can work together.

02 / Voice and atmosphere

R&B

R&B stands for rhythm and blues. It covers a wide range of music, but melody, vocal expression, and groove are useful things to listen for. The artists in this guide represent the quieter, more atmospheric side of my rotation.

Compare “In This Darkness” by Clara La San with “Break from Toronto” by PARTYNEXTDOOR. Listen to the space around the vocals, the background sounds, and how the rhythm changes the feeling of each song. Even when two songs fit a similar mood, their production can make them feel very different.
