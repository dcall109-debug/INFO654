Familiar names different feelings

These are some of my favorite artists. Each brings something different to my rotation, from rap to R&B.

01 Drake

A song for every mood

What I like about Drake is that I can listen to him in pretty much any mood. Sometimes I want to hear him rap, and other times I’m more interested in his slower songs. “Passionfruit” is a good place to start. The beat is laid-back, and the chorus is easy to get into. It’s the kind of song you can put on while hanging out and let play without thinking about skipping it.

Explore Drake on Spotify

02 Hunxho

When I want something more personal

What stands out to me about Hunxho is his voice. He mixes rapping and singing in a way that makes the songs feel personal, especially when he’s talking about relationships. I’d start with “Your Friends.” The melody catches your attention, but his delivery is what makes the song worth coming back to.

Explore Hunxho on Spotify

03 NoCap

Worth a closer listen

NoCap is someone I’d put on when I want to pay more attention to the words. His melodies make the songs easy to get into, but there’s usually a line that makes me want to rewind. “Ghetto Angels” is where I’d start. There’s a lot of emotion in his voice, and the song gives you a good idea of what makes his music stand out.

Explore NoCap on Spotify

04 Clara La San

Something to slow down to

Clara La San brings a different sound to this list. Her music feels quieter and more spacious, which is a nice change when I’ve been listening to a lot of rap. “In This Darkness” is my starting pick. Her voice and the beat work really well together without making the song feel crowded. I’d give this one a listen with headphones.

Explore Clara La San on Spotify

05 PARTYNEXTDOOR

For the late-night playlist

PARTYNEXTDOOR fits the slower side of my music taste. I like how his songs have a relaxed feel but still have enough rhythm to keep them moving. “Break from Toronto” is a good introduction. It’s short, but it gives you a feel for his sound right away. By the time it ends, it’s easy to want another listen.

Explore PARTYNEXTDOOR on Spotify
